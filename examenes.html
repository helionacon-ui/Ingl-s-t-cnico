Cómo usarlos

Cuatro de ellos son ejercicios reales de clase con su solución, y los otros cinco son las tareas que se piden en el portafolio y en la prueba escrita, con un modelo completo y el análisis de lo que puntúa.

Problema 1 · Clasificar el registro de un grupo de frases clase 1 T8

Enunciado

Classify the sentences below as formal, neutral or informal: «You got a sec?» · «Could I disturb you for a minute?» · «Can I ask you something?» · «We need to check everything before we carry on.» · «We need to verify the data before we proceed.» · «Just FYI, we’d like to fix up a meeting in October.»
✓Solución completa
FraseRegistroMarcador
You got a sec?Informalfalta el auxiliar + abreviatura sec
Could I disturb you for a minute?Formalcould + disturb
Can I ask you something?Neutralestructura completa, léxico común
…check everything before we carry on.Informalphrasal verb carry on
…verify the data before we proceed.Formaldos verbos de origen latino
Just FYI, we’d like to fix up a meeting.Informalsigla + contracción + phrasal verb

El método seguro en el examen: buscar los tres marcadores en orden — contracciones, phrasal verbs y verbos latinos.

Variante frecuente: reescribir una frase informal en registro formal. Sustituye el phrasal verb, quita la contracción y desarrolla las siglas.

Problema 2 · Propiedades de materiales a partir de descripciones clase 2 T2

Enunciado

Read the statements and complete the table with the key properties of each material: carbon fibre «doesn’t weigh very much and can be bent easily»; aramid fibre «can’t be stretched easily and can withstand a heavy blow without breaking»; polyurethane foam «is soft and reduces the effect of a violent blow without transferring the impact»; TPU «doesn’t break easily, but can be stretched or bent and return to its original shape».
✓Solución completa
MaterialKey properties
Carbon fibrelightweight · flexible
Aramid fibrestrong in tension · impact resistant
Polyurethane foamsoft · impact absorbent
TPUtough · elastic

La distinción clave: strong (soporta carga) ≠ tough (absorbe energía sin fracturarse) ≠ impact absorbent (amortigua y no transmite el golpe). Lo contrario de tough es brittle.

La segunda parte del ejercicio pide emparejar adjetivos (malleable, ductile, absorbent…) con sus definiciones: está en la figura interactiva del tema 2.

Problema 3 · Unir pares de frases con relativos clase 3 T8

Enunciado

Join these pairs of sentences using who, which, where or from where: (a) A serious air disaster occurred with TWA flight 800. It crashed into the Atlantic. (b) The last radio transmission was received at Boston. Here the weather was fine. (c) The explosion was seen by another pilot. He was flying in the area. (d) The other pilot landed at Boston airport. From here he contacted the investigators.
✓Solución completa
  1. A serious air disaster occurred with TWA flight 800, which crashed into the Atlantic.
  2. …the last radio transmission was received at Boston, where the weather was fine.
  3. The explosion was seen by another pilot, who was flying in the area at the time.
  4. The other pilot landed at Boston airport, from where he contacted the air crash investigators.

Las dos reglas que resuelven el ejercicio:

  1. Todas son explicativas (llevan coma), así que no se puede usar that.
  2. El pronombre lo decide el antecedente: cosa ⇒ which, persona ⇒ who, lugar ⇒ where, lugar con movimiento desde ⇒ from where.

La trampa habitual es poner «that» tras la coma, que en inglés cuidado se considera un error; sólo aparece en estilo descuidado.

Problema 4 · Encontrar términos en un texto técnico clase 3 T5

Enunciado

Read the text about regenerative brakes and find the words that match these definitions: (a) materials that are not metal · (b) materials such as iron and steel · (c) a combination of materials (two words) · (d) a mixture of metals · (e) plastic materials · (f) materials made of minerals transformed by heat · (g) rare or complex.
✓Solución completa
DefiniciónTérmino
materials that are not metalnon-metallic materials
materials such as iron and steelferrous metal
combination of materials (2 words)asbestos-based composites (genérico: composites)
mixture of metalsalloy
plastic materialspolymers
minerals transformed by heatceramics
rare or complexexotic

Técnica: no traduzcas la definición; localiza el lugar del texto donde se habla de materiales y fíjate en los marcadores «made of», «made from», «consisting of». Copia la palabra tal como aparece.

Si piden «two words», casi siempre es un compuesto (adjetivo + sustantivo) y hay que copiarlo entero.

Problema 5 · Resumen de un vídeo divulgativo portafolio T1

Enunciado

Write a summary (100-120 words) of a video about global electricity demand. Include the topic, the main figure, the two causes of the increase, the problems of non-fossil sources and a conclusion.
✓Solución completa

This video deals with electricity needs on a global scale. Finding out about them requires determining current consumption and future needs; an 80 % increase in demand is expected by 2050, but the actual demand may be greater. This is because of two main factors: rapid industrialisation in developing countries, and the need to switch from fossil fuels to electricity in order to mitigate climate change. However, using non-fossil fuels poses some problems: solar and wind power are difficult to store and ship, and nuclear fusion raises safety concerns, although safety is improving and research is progressing. In a nutshell, more electricity is going to be needed, which should be cleaner and produced using all available non-fossil sources.

Lo que puntúa: apertura con fórmula («This video deals with…»), cifra con marco temporal, causas enumeradas con dos puntos, contraste con However, concesión con although, cierre con In a nutshell y relativa explicativa. Y, sobre todo, paráfrasis: copiar frases del original penaliza.

Cuenta las palabras: pasarse del límite también resta. 100-120 son unas 7-8 frases.

Problema 6 · Correo profesional de solicitud portafolio T10

Enunciado

Write an email to a supplier you have never dealt with, asking for the technical specifications and a quotation for two industrial robots, with a deadline of 25 May.
✓Solución completa

Subject: Request for technical information and quotation – XR-50 robot

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to enquire about the XR-50 articulated robot, which is listed on your website as suitable for palletising applications.

We are currently evaluating options for a new packaging line and would be grateful if you could provide the following: first, the full technical specifications, including payload, reach and repeatability; and second, a quotation for two units, with delivery to Cartagena, Spain.

As the project has to be approved by the end of the month, we would appreciate it if you could send the information by 25 May. Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require any further details.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,
[Name]

Las trampas: «Dear Sir or Madam» exige Yours faithfully (no sincerely); el plazo va con by (no until); y la fórmula final es «look forward to hearing», con -ing.

Si conoces el nombre: «Dear Ms Taylor,» … «Yours sincerely,». Entre compañeros: «Hi Marta,» … «Best regards,».

Problema 7 · Describir un dispositivo prueba escrita T4

Enunciado

Describe an industrial temperature sensor (thermocouple with a 4-20 mA transmitter) in 6-8 sentences: definition, composition, operating principle, performance and a safety warning.
✓Solución completa

A thermocouple is a temperature sensor which consists of two wires of different metals joined at one end. When the junction is heated, a small voltage is generated, and this voltage varies with the temperature difference between the measuring and the reference junction. The sensor is normally fitted with a stainless-steel sheath, which protects the wires from corrosion. The signal is then fed into a transmitter that converts it into a 4-20 mA current loop, so that it can be sent over long distances without significant losses. Type K thermocouples are capable of measuring temperatures ranging from −200 to 1 260 °C, although their accuracy is lower than that of a resistance thermometer. Warning: do not install the sensor without its protective sheath; failure to do so may result in contamination of the measuring junction.

Siete estructuras evaluables: definición con relativa especificativa, condición-efecto con when, explicativa con coma, especificativa con that, propósito con so that, capacidad con be capable of + -ing y advertencia con failure to do so may result in.

Si te piden describir un objeto cualquiera, la plantilla es la misma: qué es, de qué está hecho, cómo funciona, qué prestaciones tiene y qué precauciones exige.

Problema 8 · Describir un proceso de fabricación prueba escrita T3

Enunciado

Describe the manufacturing of an aluminium housing in 8-10 sentences: melting, casting, cooling, demoulding, milling and drilling, deburring, anodising, inspection and packaging.
✓Solución completa

First, aluminium ingots are melted in a furnace at approximately 700 °C. The molten metal is then poured into a die, where it is left to solidify. Once the casting has cooled down, it is removed from the mould. After that, the part is machined: the mating surfaces are milled and the mounting holes are drilled. The edges are deburred in order to remove any sharp burrs left by machining. The housing is subsequently anodised, which provides a hard, corrosion-resistant surface. Each unit is then inspected and the critical dimensions are checked against the drawing tolerances. Finally, the parts that pass inspection are packaged, while rejected units are recycled.

Lo que se evalúa: pasiva presente en todas las frases de proceso, variedad de conectores sin repetir then, propósito con in order to, explicativa con which, preposiciones correctas (at 700 °C, against the tolerances) y un contraste final con while.

En la versión oral se mantiene la estructura pero se añaden marcadores de voz alta: «Let’s start with…», «Once that’s done…».

Problema 9 · Guion de la presentación en grupo 20 % de la nota T9

Enunciado

Prepare the script of a 10-minute group presentation (three speakers) on «Regenerative braking in Formula 1», with explicit transitions and a prepared Q&A.
✓Solución completa
MomentoFórmula
Apertura«Good morning everyone, and thank you for coming. We’re going to talk about…»
Estructura«We’ve divided our talk into three parts: first… then… and finally…»
Visual«As you can see from this diagram, friction generates heat, which is wasted.»
Relevo«That brings me to the alternative. Now I’ll hand over to Marta, who will explain…»
Énfasis«The key point here is that…» · «I’d like to stress that…»
Cierre«To sum up, three ideas: … Thank you for your attention. We’d be happy to answer any questions.»
Pregunta difícil«I’m afraid I don’t have that figure to hand, but I’d be glad to send it to you.»

Reparto del tiempo: 1 minuto de apertura y estructura, 3 + 3 + 2 de contenido y 1 de cierre. Si hay que recortar, se recorta el desarrollo, nunca el cierre.

Lo que más penaliza es leer la diapositiva. Ensaya en voz alta tres veces y cronometra.
