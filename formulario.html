Formulario

Definir y describir

  • A ___ is a device which / that …
  • It is used for measuring… / used to measure…
  • It consists of … / It comprises … (sin of)
  • It is made of steel / made from wood
  • It is fitted with … / attached to …
  • It is capable of lifting up to 50 kg
  • Its purpose is to… / It enables the operator to…

Procesos y secuencia

  • Pasiva presente: «the parts are welded»
  • First… Then / Next… After that… Finally…
  • Once the metal has cooled, …
  • While / As the reaction takes place…
  • in order to + infinitivo (propósito)
  • As a result / Consequently / Therefore
  • However, … (siempre con coma)

Relativas

  • Especificativa (sin coma): which / that / zero
  • Explicativa (con coma): which / who / where · nunca that
  • Personas: who · cosas: which · lugar: where
  • Posesivo: whose · formal: whom
  • Preposición + relativo: from where, in which
  • «the sensor that failed» ≠ «the sensor, which failed,»

Condicionales y causa-efecto

  • Tipo 0: If + presente, presente (leyes generales)
  • Tipo 1: If + presente, will (caso futuro)
  • Tipo 2: If + pasado, would (hipótesis)
  • Should both switches fail, … (inversión formal)
  • In the event of + sustantivo
  • This causes / leads to / results in …
  • Due to / Owing to + sustantivo

Cifras y especificaciones

  • up to 50 kg · within ±0,02 mm
  • ranging from −10 to 60 °C
  • an increase of 80 % · to rise by 80 %
  • 40 % of the energy (sin artículo)
  • approximately / in excess of / at least
  • a six-axis robot (con guion, singular)
  • 1 260 °C se lee «one thousand two hundred and sixty degrees»

Registro

  • Informal: contracciones, phrasal verbs, siglas (FYI)
  • Formal: verbos latinos, sin contracciones
  • check → verify · carry on → proceed
  • get → obtain · ask for → request
  • find out → determine · fix up → arrange
  • but → however · so → therefore · about → regarding

Correos

  • Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully
  • Dear Mr/Ms + apellido → Yours sincerely
  • Dear + nombre → Best regards
  • I am writing to enquire about / with regard to…
  • I would be grateful if you could…
  • Please find attached… · by 25 May (≠ until)
  • I look forward to hearing from you (con -ing)

Presentaciones

  • Good morning everyone, and thank you for coming.
  • I’ve divided my talk into three parts.
  • That brings me to my next point.
  • As you can see from this graph…
  • I’d like to stress that… / The key point here is…
  • Now I’ll hand over to my colleague…
  • To sum up… Thank you for your attention.

Falsos amigos

  • actually = en realidad → actualmente es currently
  • eventually = finalmente → eventualmente es occasionally
  • assist = ayudar → asistir a es attend
  • sensible = sensato → sensible es sensitive
  • fabric = tejido → fábrica es factory / plant
  • realise = darse cuenta → realizar es carry out
  • resume = reanudar → resumir es summarise
  • tension = tensión mecánica → eléctrica es voltage

Incontables y plurales

  • equipment, machinery, information, advice, research, software
  • «a piece of equipment», «some information»
  • data: data are (clásico) o data is (moderno): sé coherente
  • siglas en plural sin apóstrofo: PCBs, LEDs
  • analysis → analyses · criterion → criteria
  • un «six-axis robot» tiene «six axes»
